# Keep the agency's own lead flow ahead of the work it needs

Key results:

- Lead to client conversion. KPI `kpi.professional-services.lead-to-client-conversion-rate`. Target: the quarterly target. Direction: up. Cadence: month.
- Wins with a known source. KPI `kpi.marketing-and-communications.wins-by-lead-source`. Target: every win coded to a source. Direction: up. Cadence: month.

Anti-pattern: a key result that is only a task, or a target with no KPI id.
