# Ship the release without a critical regression

Key results:

- Critical bugs after release. KPI `kpi.information-technology.critical-defects`. Target: at most 1. Direction: down. Cadence: quarter.
- Regressions. KPI `kpi.information-technology.regressions`. Target: zero. Direction: down. Cadence: quarter.

Anti-pattern: a key result that is only a task, or a target with no KPI id.
