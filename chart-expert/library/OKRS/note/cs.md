# See risk before the customer leaves

Key results:

- Accounts with a health score. KPI `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.accounts-with-health-score`. Target: every active account. Direction: up. Cadence: week.
- At-risk accounts contacted. KPI `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.at-risk-accounts-contacted`. Target: every account flagged that week. Direction: up. Cadence: week.

Anti-pattern: a key result that is only a task, or a target with no KPI id.
