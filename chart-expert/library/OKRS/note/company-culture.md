# Build a culture people want to stay in

Key results:

- Run a weekly pulse. KPI `kpi.human-resources.employee-pulse-score`. Target: 8 or higher. Direction: up. Cadence: week.
- Hold a monthly town hall with open questions. KPI `kpi.human-resources.town-hall-held`. Target: 1 per month. Direction: up. Cadence: month.

Anti-pattern: a key result that is only a task, or a target with no KPI id.
