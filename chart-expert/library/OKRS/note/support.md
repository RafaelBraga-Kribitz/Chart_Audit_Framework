# Resolve support at the standard the customer was promised

Key results:

- Tier-1 CSAT. KPI `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.customer-satisfaction-score`. Target: 90 percent or higher. Direction: up. Cadence: week.
- Tier-1 first response. KPI `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.first-response-time`. Target: within 1 hour at the 90th percentile. Direction: down. Cadence: week.
- Tier-2 resolved in a day. KPI `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.tickets-resolved-within-24-hours`. Target: 95 percent. Direction: up. Cadence: week.
