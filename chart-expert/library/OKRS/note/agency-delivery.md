# Deliver projects inside the hours and cost we quoted

Key results:

- Hours versus estimate. KPI `kpi.portfolio-and-project-management.estimated-versus-actual-project-time`. Target: inside the agreed band, for example 100 to 110 (estimate over actual). Direction: corridor. Cadence: project.
- Cost versus estimate. KPI `kpi.portfolio-and-project-management.estimated-versus-actual-project-cost`. Target: inside the agreed band, for example 100 to 110 (estimate over actual). Direction: corridor. Cadence: project.
- Contribution margin. KPI `kpi.portfolio-and-project-management.project-contribution-margin`. Target: at or above the quoted margin. Direction: up. Cadence: project.

Anti-pattern: a key result that is only a task, or a target with no KPI id.
