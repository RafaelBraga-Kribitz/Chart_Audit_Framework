# 17. NPS with the distribution

Question: What sits behind the single NPS figure?

Charts: `diverging-stacked-bar`, `big-number`.

Why: The score for the executive, the distribution for the researcher.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 17. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
