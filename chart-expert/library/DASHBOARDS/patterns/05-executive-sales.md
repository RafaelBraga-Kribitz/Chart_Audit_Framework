# 5. Executive sales

Question: Are we on pace, and which segment explains the gap?

Charts: `line-chart`, `bar-chart`, `waterfall-chart`.

Why: Score, trend, then one breakdown.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 5. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
