# 30. Personal scope

Question: Does this page show the viewer's own work?

Charts: `data-table`, `big-number`.

Why: Impersonal walls do not get action.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 30. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
