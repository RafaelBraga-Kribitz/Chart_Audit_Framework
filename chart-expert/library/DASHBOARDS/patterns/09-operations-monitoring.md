# 9. Operations monitoring

Question: Is the process inside its corridor right now?

Charts: `run-chart`, `control-chart`.

Why: Technical audience may use the control chart. The shift lead may only need the run and the exceptions.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 9. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
