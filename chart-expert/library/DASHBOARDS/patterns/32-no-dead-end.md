# 32. No dead end

Question: Can the reader reach the entity they must change?

Charts: `bar-chart`, `data-table`.

Why: Score without a detail zone is a dead end.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 32. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
