# 25. Actual versus potential

Question: How far is actual from the ceiling we believe?

Charts: `bullet-graph`, `diverging-bar`.

Why: State what potential means.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 25. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
