# 16. Sentiment distribution

Question: Is the average hiding an angry tail?

Charts: `diverging-stacked-bar`, `histogram`.

Why: Show the distribution. The mean is not the story.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 16. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
