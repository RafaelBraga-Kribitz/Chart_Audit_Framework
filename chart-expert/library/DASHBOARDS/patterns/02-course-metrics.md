# 2. Course metrics

Question: Is the cohort learning, and where is the drop?

Charts: `line-chart`, `bar-chart`.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 2. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
