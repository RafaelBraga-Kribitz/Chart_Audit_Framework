# 4. What-if

Question: What happens to the result if one driver moves?

Charts: `kpi-tree`, `waterfall-chart`.

Why: The tree is the model. The waterfall is the result of one change.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 4. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
