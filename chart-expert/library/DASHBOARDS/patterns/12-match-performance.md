# 12. Match performance

Question: What changed during the event?

Charts: `timeline`, `bar-chart`.

Why: Time order of events, then the totals.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 12. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
