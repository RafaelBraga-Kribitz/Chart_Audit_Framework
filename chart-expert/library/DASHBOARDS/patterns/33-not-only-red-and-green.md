# 33. Not only red and green

Question: Can the variance be read without color?

Charts: `diverging-bar`.

Why: Sign and position carry the message.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 33. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
