# 35. Not a cloud of bubbles

Question: Is area pretending to be a precise magnitude?

Charts: `scatter-plot`, `bar-chart`.

Why: Bubbles only when a third variable is real and the audience is analytical.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 35. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
