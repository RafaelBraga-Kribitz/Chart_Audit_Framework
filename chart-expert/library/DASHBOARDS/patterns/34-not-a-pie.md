# 34. Not a pie

Question: Is a part-to-whole being shown with angles?

Charts: `stacked-bar-chart`.

Why: Bars or a table of shares.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 34. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
