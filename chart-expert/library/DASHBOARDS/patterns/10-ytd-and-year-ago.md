# 10. YTD and year ago

Question: How does this year compare with last year, to date?

Charts: `line-chart`, `diverging-bar`.

Why: Two explicit series. Do not make the reader remember.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 10. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
