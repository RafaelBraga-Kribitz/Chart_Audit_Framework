# 20. Complaints

Question: What do people complain about, and is it growing?

Charts: `pareto-chart`, `line-chart`.

Why: Pareto of reasons, trend of volume.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 20. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
