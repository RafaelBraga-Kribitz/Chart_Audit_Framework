# 18. Server monitoring

Question: Which process is away from its corridor?

Charts: `run-chart`, `horizon-chart`.

Why: Horizon is a notebook or an operator wall, not a board slide.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 18. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
