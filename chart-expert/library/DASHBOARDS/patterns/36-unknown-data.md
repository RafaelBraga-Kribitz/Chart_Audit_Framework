# 36. Unknown data

Question: Do we know the shape before we pick the chart?

Charts: `histogram`, `scatter-plot`.

Why: Look first. A dashboard template is not a finding.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 36. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
