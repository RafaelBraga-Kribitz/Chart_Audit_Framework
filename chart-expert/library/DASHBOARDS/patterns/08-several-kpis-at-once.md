# 8. Several KPIs at once

Question: Are the few key results healthy together?

Charts: `ibcs-variance-table`, `bullet-graph`.

Why: A scorecard, not forty tiles.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 8. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
