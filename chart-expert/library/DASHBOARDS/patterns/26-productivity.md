# 26. Productivity

Question: Output per person, with the mix visible?

Charts: `bar-chart`, `scatter-plot`.

Why: Scatter for the analyst. Bar of the outliers for the manager.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 26. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
