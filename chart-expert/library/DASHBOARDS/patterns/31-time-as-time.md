# 31. Time as time

Question: Is time an axis?

Charts: `line-chart`.

Why: Do not encode time only as color or a pie slice.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 31. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
