# 24. Churn

Question: Who left, and was it logos or revenue?

Charts: `waterfall-chart`, `line-chart`.

Why: Do not mix the two definitions.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 24. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
