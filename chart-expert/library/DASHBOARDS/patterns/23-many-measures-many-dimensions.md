# 23. Many measures, many dimensions

Question: Which slice is different on more than one measure?

Charts: `ibcs-variance-table`, `heat-map`.

Why: A table with marks. Not a radar.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 23. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
