# 7. Pace to goal

Question: Will we land on the target if the recent rate holds?

Charts: `line-chart`, `bullet-graph`.

Why: Path plus target. A gauge throws the path away.

Chapter reference: The Big Book of Dashboards (Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave; Wiley, 2017), chapter 7. The card is a rule, not the chapter.

Audience split: the analytical form may be a notebook plot. The communication form is the simpler chart in the list.
