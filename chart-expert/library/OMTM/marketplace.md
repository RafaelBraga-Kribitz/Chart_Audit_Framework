# Marketplace

A marketplace steers on whether both sides of the market are transacting.

KPI: `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.marketplace-liquidity` (Marketplace liquidity, percent, direction up). Formula `(A / B) * 100`.

Communication surface: `bullet-graph` on a dashboard or board pack. Analysis surface: `histogram` in a notebook.
