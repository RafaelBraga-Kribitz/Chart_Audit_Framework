# Ecommerce

A store steers on conversion once traffic is a known input, not on traffic alone.

KPI: `kpi.online-presence.ecommerce-conversion-rate` (Ecommerce conversion rate, percent, direction up). Formula `(A / B) * 100`.

Communication surface: `bullet-graph` on a dashboard or board pack. Analysis surface: `histogram` in a notebook.
