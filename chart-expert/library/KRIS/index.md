# Key risk indicators

5 measures use `measure_kind: kri`. They track exposure, not success. Generated by `chart-expert/scripts/build_measures.py`. Do not edit by hand.

- `kpi.finance.accounts-receivable-aging` Accounts receivable aging (down, currency). Communication `stacked-bar-chart`.
- `kpi.information-technology.critical-defects` Critical defects (down, count). Communication `run-chart`.
- `kpi.information-technology.regressions` Regressions (down, count). Communication `run-chart`.
- `kpi.information-technology.unplanned-downtime` Unplanned downtime (down, hours). Communication `run-chart`.
- `kpi.sales-and-customer-service.customer-churn-rate` Customer churn rate (down, percent). Communication `line-chart`.
