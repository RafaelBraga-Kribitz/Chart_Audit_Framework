When you want show where your data points are located on a map.

For example if **“you would like to show …”**

… the *route* between two location you can choose a **route map**.

… the *number of people* that live in an region you can choose a **chloropeth map**.

… the *distribution* of supermarkets in a region, you can choose a **dot map**.
