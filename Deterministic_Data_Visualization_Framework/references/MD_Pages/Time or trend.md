When you want show the change of category A and B over time.

For example if “ **you would like to show …** ”

… how the *revenue per month* generated by the members of your sales department over the last quarter, you can use a **linechart**.

… how the ranking of a sports competition is changed to last year, you can use a **slopegraph**.

… how a single KPI’s scores better or worse then its target, for every single month in the last year, you can use a **deviation column chart**.
